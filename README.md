# Welcome, friend, to the Summoner's Guild!
Don't scuff the chalk, please.

## Who we are
The Summoner's Guild started as a small group of friends in high school with a wildly unrealistic dream: to make video games. Being based in "flyover country" as we are, though, we were faced with a dilemma: how are we supposed to make games if there are no game studios around? Shove off for the coast, perhaps, but we like our families too much to live halfway across the country from them. So, we made our own! Now, we're a small team of independent game designers based out of the Kansas City Metropolitan Area, pooling our (questionable) talents to create wacky, somewhat unhinged, hopefully-memorable experiences for players to enjoy. Probably.

## Our games
We're working on our first major release right now. It's not quite ready for the public eye just yet (digital paint takes forever to dry), but the development ritual is well underway, so stick around!
